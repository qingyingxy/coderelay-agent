## Mechanism And Cost Checks

- All 18 hard-cut runs used Notes and issued a successful History query.
- All 12 C/D runs persisted and referenced a Workflow Snapshot at the boundary.
- All 24 runs retained the original Session JSONL while excluding the old lookup result from active history after the boundary.
- The matrix made 140 provider calls and used 475,966 input, 11,272 output, 3,836 reasoning, and 863,744 cache-read tokens. Reasoning is a subset of output and was not charged twice.
- The Artifact contains 24 Session JSONL files, 24 traces, 24 final responses, and 24 result files under three separate repeat directories.
- The current matrix cost $1.259945. Combined with the previously recorded CW.17 development total of approximately $1.183453, known evaluation spend is approximately $2.443398 at the pinned OpenAI price basis.

## Decision

The namespace fix is validated for this controlled task set. The result also shows that hard-cut strategies can match the summary baseline while reducing input tokens and estimated cost, with B cheapest in this sample. It does not establish that C or D is better than A on broad long-running coding work, because the suite has only two synthetic tasks and one forced boundary per run.

Keep `summary` as the default. Before CW.18, expand evaluation to multi-window coding tasks, resume/fork/rollback, failed Attempt repair, overflow recovery, and branch-isolation cases.
