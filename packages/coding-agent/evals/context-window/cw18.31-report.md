## What This Establishes

The natural runner and independent verifier work on this candidate. Both
trajectories ended at under 50K request input and in roughly five to six minutes
of SDK execution. The limits did not truncate them. The task's sync/async/
streaming complexity therefore did not produce a naturally long trajectory in
these observations. Complexity of requirements alone was an insufficient
screening signal.

Retain this as a failed task-quality pair with no recovery coverage. Do not lower
the window or inject a boundary afterward and relabel it as natural behavior.
CW18.29 remains the separate controlled-recovery baseline.

Further natural-long-task selection should prioritize evidence of previously
observed long trajectories or a real sustained multi-stage workload, rather
than another task selected only from its description or test count. No further
task was automatically launched and no routing conclusion was drawn here.
